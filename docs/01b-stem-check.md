# GravitySort STEM check

Verdict: no claim fails outright. Of 40 claims, 29 sit within known science after five fixes made under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23; 9 are plausible but need a test or a datasheet at TRL 4 (fine-gold retention in the bed, critical speed on the real frame, table ratio, the motor's speed cap, filter performance, liner casting and wear, borax smelting of a sulfide-rich concentrate, option C's hand panning); and 2 fail with a fix proposed that goes to Amish because each fix breaks the 100 kg of PR-011 (containment of a bowl fragment, S10; the full tank's sway near the table drive frequency, S33). So `stem_check` is **open**. Method and rules: STANDARDS section 20. Calculations: `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py` (writes `stem_results.csv`), which imports the calculation note GVS-CAL-001 (`sizing.py`) so that every row on the TRL 3 design closes against the same numbers.

## Fixes made under the standing instruction (GVS-DEC-001)

This repo was already at TRL 3 when its first STEM check ran (the kit 1.8 upgrade), so it keeps its TRL and the fix rule applies to the failed items. Before the fixes these five rows were **Fails, fix proposed**; each fix keeps the working principle, the pitch and the scope, so it was made in the same session and the row closed (S4 was found by the independent review):

| Row | What failed | Fix (GVS-DEC-001) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S20 | The header drum was drawn 380 mm across and 400 mm tall: 45 L, not the 60 L used everywhere else | F1: drum drawn at its real 560 mm height; head and tipping rechecked (S4, S11) |
| S24 | Recirculated, silty pond water went unfiltered to 1.0 mm fluidization holes; fluidized concentrators need clean fluidization water | F2: 3/4 in screen filter, 120 mesh, with a flush valve; supply rechecked with its loss (S4, S5) |
| S16 | The BOM's buffer rating (600 N) was barely above the peak force at the largest setting (594 N) | F3: buffer rated 800 N or more, as GVS-CAL-001 section 9 had chosen |
| S21 | The gold balance used 8 h of feed although the restated throughput has 7.75 h | F4: gold balance on the feed time (7.75 g in per shift) |
| S4 | The supply calculation left out the climb from the union to the rings (4.5 to 5.6 kPa); with it the lowest ring had 6.0 kPa net at 600 rpm against the proposed 10 kPa | F6: ball valve and rotameter with 3/4 in bores (fittings loss 7.5 to 3.4 kPa); 10.0 kPa at mid-tank, 7.0 kPa with the tank nearly empty and the filter due for flushing |

The detailed CAD of the kit 1.9.1 upgrade found one more clash, not a science row: the head pillow block nuts hit a shelf gusset, fixed by moving the gusset (F5). Two rows fail with fixes that break PR-011 and therefore go to Amish: S10 (containment, GVS-DEC-001 O2) and S33 (tank sway, O3).

## Fields not relevant

- Physics, electromagnetism and circuits: GravitySort has no circuit of its own beyond a bicycle computer; the motor option's electrics are MotionCore's and are checked in that repository.
- Physics, optics and radiation; nuclear and ionising radiation: nothing in the design emits or depends on light or radiation.
- Chemistry, electrochemistry and batteries: only through the MotionCore pack (MotionCore repository); galvanic corrosion of the stainless spindle in the painted steel frame is a maintenance point, not a performance claim.
- Chemistry, organic and analytical: no reagents beyond water; borax is used at the smelting step outside the machine (S27).
- Biology, microbiology, plant and animal biology, biochemistry: no living material in the process; mosquitoes in ponds are covered under epidemiology (S30).

## Concept options

- **Option A, fluidized bowl and shaking table (current design):** no row fails outright; S10 (containment) and S33 (tank sway) fail with fixes that go to Amish; plausible rows S3, S8, S15, S23, S24, S25, S27 and S29 need tests or datasheets.
- **Option B, diaphragm jig and shaking table:** no row fails (B1 to B4). A first model of the jig with a 124 mm diaphragm gave only about 2 mm of water stroke over the bed, below what pulses the ragging; the option was modelled with a 190 mm plate in a 216 mm opening (5.7 mm) before it was scored. Jigs lose much of the gold finer than about 0.1 mm (B3), which is scored against UR-003 in GVS-OPT-001.
- **Option C, carpeted sluice and gold pans:** no row fails (C1 to C3); sluices recover less than 20 % of gold finer than 100 um (C1), which is scored against UR-003, and the panning labour is plausible on paper only (C3).
